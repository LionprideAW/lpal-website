In loving memory

Ethan Lovei

2007 2026

Armwrestler France

Some athletes only need a few moments on stage to leave a legacy behind that will live forever.

We had the honour and privilege of getting to know Ethan and sharing two unforgettable events with him. Though our time together was far too short, the memories he gave us will stay in our hearts forever.

He walked onto the stage with a smile that could light up the room, pulled with all his heart, and walked away with the love, respect, and cheers of an entire hall behind him.

Ethan was more than just an athlete on our stage. He became part of our family, part of our story, and forever part of the Lion Pride.

This is more than a story of strength. This is a story of heart, courage, and a young man who left a lasting mark on everyone who had the privilege of knowing him.

This is the story of Ethan Lovei.

Chapter I

LPAL 2

3 May 2025 Stage3, Vienna

His first time on the LPAL stage. In the Lightweight division, Ethan took on Nemanja Milanovic and pushed him all the way: five rounds, decided 3:2.

Face to face

Smiling at the table in the middle of the battle against Nemanja Milanovic.

Quiet strength

Calm and humble, with a fire inside that everyone around him could feel.

Five rounds. He left everything he had that night on the table.

Chapter II

LPAL 3

31 January 2026 Stage3, Vienna

Ethan came back to Vienna, wearing the colours of France on his sleeve. Against Sup1nator he pulled with everything he had and won 3:0.

That energy was Ethan.

His father was always by his side: his biggest support and his greatest inspiration in the sport.

A warm smile, a big heart, and the whole hall behind him.
