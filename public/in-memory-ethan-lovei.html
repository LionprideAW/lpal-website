In loving memory

Ethan Lovei

Armwrestler France

Some athletes only need a few moments on stage to leave a legacy behind that will live forever.

We had the privilege of getting to know Ethan and sharing two events with him, and the memories he gave us will last far beyond our lifetime. He walked out with a smile, pulled with his whole heart and left the stage with the whole hall behind him. This is the story of Ethan Lovei.

Chapter I

LPAL 2

3 May 2025 Stage3, Vienna

His first time on the LPAL stage. In the Lightweight division, Ethan took on Nemanja Milanovic and pushed him all the way: five rounds, decided 3:2.

Face to face

Smiling at the table in the middle of the battle against Nemanja Milanovic.

Quiet strength

Calm and humble, with a fire inside that everyone around him could feel.

Five rounds. Full heart in every one of them.

Chapter II

LPAL 3

31 January 2026 Stage3, Vienna

Ethan came back to Vienna, wearing the colours of France on his sleeve. Against Sup1nator he pulled with everything he had and won 3:0.

That energy was Ethan.

His father was always by his side: his biggest support and his greatest inspiration in the sport.

A warm smile, a big heart, and the whole hall behind him.

Ethan Lovei

Though you may no longer be by our side, you will forever live in our hearts. Your spirit, your strength, and the memories you leave behind will always be part of us.
