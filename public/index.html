    For athletes

    Road to LPAL

    Every LPAL event starts long before the lights go down. The LPAL Qualifier is open to athletes who want a place in the league.

    How do I qualify and get the chance to be a part of the Lion Pride?

    1. Morning

      The qualifier tournament

      On the day of every LPAL event, the LPAL Qualifier tournament starts in the morning, hours before the evening show.

    2. Evening

      Finals on the main show

      Depending on the event, the 1 to 3 most exciting tournament finals are selected and pulled on the main show as the event's opening matches.

    3. Qualified

      Win and you're in

      Whoever wins one of those selected matches is officially qualified for the upcoming LPAL events.

    Want to pull at the next qualifier? Send us a message on Instagram.
