    Contest rules

    The rules

    1. LPAL content only. Use only footage and photos from Lion Pride Armwrestling League (our events, YouTube, Instagram and TikTok). Edits with content from other leagues or events don't count.
    2. Logo and watermark. Every edit must show the LPAL 4 logo and the Kick watermark (kick.com/lpal_kick) from this page, both clearly visible for the whole video.
    3. Caption. Every edit must include the Kick line from this page, tag @lion_pride_armwrestling_league and use #LPALedit.
    4. Where and when. Post your edit as a public Instagram Reel or TikTok between joining and 20 December 2026, 23:59 (German time). Keep it online until the winners are announced.
    5. Your own work. Only submit edits you made yourself, from your own account. One profile per person, up to 30 links.
    6. Views. The LPAL team checks the public view count of each link. For the grand prize we count the views of all your valid edits on 21 December 2026. We may ask you for a screen recording of your statistics.
    7. Fair play. Bought views, bots, fake accounts or stolen edits mean disqualification. LPAL decides on all doubtful cases.
    8. Music and rights. Use music that the platform allows. You are responsible for the music in your edit. LPAL may repost edits with credit if you agreed to it.
    9. Prizes. First to 1,000,000 views: an LPAL merch package chosen by LPAL. Grand prize for a winner living in Europe: one VIP ticket for LPAL 4 on 9 January 2027 and an economy return flight to Cologne, booked by LPAL. Hotel and local travel are not included, and the travel prize can't be exchanged for cash. Grand prize for a winner living outside Europe: €300, paid by bank transfer or PayPal, and an LPAL 4 merch package. Winners under 18 need a guardian's consent and travel with a guardian; the guardian's costs are not included.
    10. Winners. Winners are contacted by email or by a DM from our official account @lion_pride_armwrestling_league and must answer within 5 days. LPAL never asks for passwords or payments.
    11. Organiser. Lion Pride Armwrestling League e.U., Vienna, Austria (imprint). This contest is not sponsored or run by Instagram, Meta, TikTok or Kick. Your data is only used for this contest and deleted 6 months after it ends (privacy policy).
