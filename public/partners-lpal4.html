Where your brand appears

Not just a logo on a wall

01

On every athlete's profile

Every official LPAL 4 poster is posted as a collaboration with all athletes on the card. One post, published on every big profile at once, including accounts with more than 1 million followers. Partner logos are on every poster.

02

Inside the athletes' content

Every athlete has a contract with a set number of LPAL posts. For our top partners we build your commercial into these reels, so your brand speaks to the athletes' own fans. Reserved for the Presenting and Premium partners.

03

A WWE-style walkout show

LPAL is more than armwrestling: every athlete walks out with music, lights, pyro and a custom walkout graphic on the big screen. Your brand can be part of every single walkout.

04

On the big LED screen

A large LED wall behind the stage shows athlete graphics, replays and partner logos all night, in the hall and on the livestream.

05

On almost every stream graphic

The Presenting Partner is on almost every graphic of the livestream: scoreboard, athlete cards, lower thirds and breaks. Free on Kick, worldwide.

In every clip for a year

Every match is cut into its own clip and posted by the league and the athletes. Table, jerseys and LED wall are in every frame, for 12 months and beyond.
