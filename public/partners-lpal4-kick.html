    How KICK would appear

    01

    On every athlete's profile

    Every official LPAL 4 poster is posted as a collaboration with all athletes on the card. One post, published on every big profile at once, including accounts with more than 1 million followers. KICK would be on every poster.

    02

    Inside the athletes' content

    Every athlete has a contract with a set number of LPAL posts. We would build KICK into these reels, so KICK speaks directly to the athletes' own fans.

    03

    A WWE-style walkout show

    LPAL is more than armwrestling: every athlete walks out with music, lights, pyro, a custom walkout graphic in the livestream and the athlete on the big screen in the arena. KICK would be part of every single walkout.

    04

    On the LED screen behind the athletes

    The LED screen behind the athletes is in the main camera shot all night, and behind every athlete during the walkouts. It shows athlete graphics, replays and the KICK logo, in the hall and on the livestream.

    05

    On almost every stream graphic

    KICK would be on almost every graphic of the livestream: scoreboard, athlete cards, lower thirds and breaks. Live and free on KICK, worldwide.

    06

    In every clip, for good

    Every match is cut into its own clip and posted by the league and the athletes. Table, jerseys and LED screen are in every frame, and the clips stay online with no time limit.

    The table

    The most-watched spot of the night

    Every match, every close-up and every replay happens here. This 3D model shows the LPAL competition table. Drag to spin it and pick a zone to see where the KICK logo would go. As presenting partner, KICK's logo goes on the table top, seen in every match and every close-up.
