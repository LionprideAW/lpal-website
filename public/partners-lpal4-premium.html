Where your brand appears

Not just a logo on a wall

01

On every athlete's profile

Every official LPAL 4 poster is posted as a collaboration with all athletes on the card. One post, published on every big profile at once, including accounts with more than 1 million followers. Partner logos are on every poster.

02

Inside the athletes' content

Every athlete has a contract with a set number of LPAL posts. For our top partners we build your commercial into these reels, so your brand speaks to the athletes' own fans. Reserved for our Presenting, Official Drink, Official Supplement and Official Category partners.

03

A WWE-style walkout show

LPAL is more than armwrestling: every athlete walks out with music, lights, pyro, a custom walkout graphic in the livestream and the athlete on the big screen in the arena. Your brand can be part of every single walkout.

04

On the LED screen behind the athletes

The LED screen behind the athletes is in the main camera shot all night, and behind every athlete during the walkouts. It shows athlete graphics, replays and partner logos, in the hall and on the livestream.

05

On almost every stream graphic

The Presenting Partner is on almost every graphic of the livestream: scoreboard, athlete cards, lower thirds and breaks. Free on Kick, worldwide.

06

In every clip, for good

Every match is cut into its own clip and posted by the league and the athletes. Table, jerseys and LED screen are in every frame, and the clips stay online with no time limit.
