What's at stake

From the tournament to the LPAL 4 stage

4athletes are picked for the prelims of LPAL 4

Our scouting team watches the whole tournament and picks 4 athletes for super matches in the prelims of the LPAL 4 evening show.

That can be a tournament final, or the winners of two different weight classes, for example the −85 kg winner against the −95 kg winner. The scouting team decides on the day of the event.

Only the Senior men's classes and the women's classes can qualify for the league later.

Good to know

Rules

  • Too heavy? If you weigh more than your class, you move up to the next heavier class.
  • Tolerance: 1 kg, only at the weigh-in on Saturday at the venue. No tolerance at the hotel on Friday.
  • No minimum age. Athletes under 18 need a parent or legal guardian (18+) who signs for them.
  • Bring your ID to the weigh-in.
  • Refunds: the 4 athletes who are selected for the super matches get their tournament entry and their LPAL 4 ticket refunded.
