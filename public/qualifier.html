What's at stake

From the tournament to the LPAL 4 stage

4athletes are picked for the prelims of LPAL 4

Our scouting team watches the whole tournament and picks 4 athletes for super matches in the prelims of the LPAL 4 evening show.

That can be a tournament final, or the winners of two different weight classes, for example the −85 kg winner against the −95 kg winner. The scouting team decides on the day of the event.

Only the Senior men's classes and the women's classes can qualify for the league later.

1. Taking part at your own risk

Armwrestling is a contact sport with a real risk of injury, including injuries to the arm, elbow, shoulder and wrist, and in rare cases broken bones (for example of the upper arm). By registering, you confirm that you know these risks and take part voluntarily and at your own risk.

You confirm that you are healthy and fit to compete, and that no doctor has advised you against strength sports. If you have a medical condition or a recent injury, do not take part, or speak to your doctor first.

LPAL is liable only for damage caused intentionally or through gross negligence by LPAL or its helpers. Liability for injury to life, body or health caused by a culpable breach of duty by LPAL is not excluded. LPAL is not liable for injuries caused by your own actions, by your opponent, or by risks typical of the sport.

We strongly recommend that you have your own health and accident insurance. First aid is available at the venue.

2. Rules, weigh-in and referees

  • The tournament follows the LPAL rules. The referees' decisions are final.
  • Every athlete must weigh in on Friday 8 January (18:00–21:00, V8 Hotel Köln @ MOTORWORLD) or Saturday 9 January (09:00–11:00, MOTORWORLD), with a photo ID.
  • If you weigh more than your class, you move up to the next heavier class. A tolerance of 1 kg applies only at the Saturday weigh-in.
  • Unsportsmanlike behaviour, aggression, or being under the influence of alcohol or drugs leads to disqualification without refund.
  • The schedule, classes and format can change on the day if needed for the event.

3. Athletes under 18

There is no minimum age. Athletes under 18 on 9 January 2027 may only take part with the written consent of a parent or legal guardian.

  • The parent or legal guardian confirms the consent online during registration.
  • At the weigh-in, the athlete must also bring a signed consent letter from the parent or legal guardian, with their name, phone number and a copy of their ID.
  • A coach or another adult may accompany the athlete, but the consent must come from the parent or legal guardian.

Without this consent, the athlete cannot compete.

4. Photos, videos, live stream

The tournament is filmed, photographed and streamed live on LPAL's Kick channel. By registering, you agree that:

  • your name, nickname, country, weight class and results may be shown in the live stream, on screens in the hall and in published results;
  • photos and videos of you may be used, without payment and without time limit, to report on and promote this event and future Lion Pride Armwrestling League events, on the LPAL website, social media (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Kick and others), posters, and by LPAL's partners and sponsors in connection with LPAL.

5. Refunds

The 4 athletes who are selected for the super matches at LPAL 4 get their tournament entry and their LPAL 4 ticket refunded. [Other cancellations: to be decided by LPAL, for example "no refunds" or "refund until a date".] If LPAL cancels the tournament, you get your tournament entry back.

6. Your data (privacy)

For the registration we collect: first and last name, email, date of birth (age), country, weight class, arm, Instagram and club (optional), your comment, and for athletes under 18 the name and email of the parent or legal guardian. If you buy a bundle or already have an LPAL 4 ticket, we also process your ticket details.

  • Why: to organise the tournament (registration, weigh-in, brackets, results, contact with you) and, as described in point 4, for the live stream and promotion.
  • Who processes it: LPAL, and the services we use for this: Stripe (payment), Ticket Tailor (tickets) and Cloudflare (website and database). Card details are handled only by Stripe; LPAL never sees them.
  • How long: registration data is deleted at the latest [12 months] after the event, unless the law requires us to keep it longer (for example payment records for tax purposes). Published results and media (point 4) stay online.
  • Your rights: you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data at any time, and you can complain to the data protection authority. Contact: business@lionpride-armwrestling.com.

7. Acceptance

By ticking the box and paying the entry fee, you confirm that you have read and accept this participation policy. For athletes under 18, the parent or legal guardian accepts it on their behalf.
